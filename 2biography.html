Biography

My name is Kimberly Zelaya-Gonzalez. I am a seventeen year old junior in highschool. I was born in Baton Rouge, Louisiana and I am also Nicaraguan as well as Honduran, and I very much love my culture. I am passionate about music and I like to play my instruments in my free time. I’m currently first chair clarinet in the Denham Springs's Wind Symphony. I play oboe as a secondary in the Symphonic Band. In previous years, I have played the Alto and Tenor Saxophone in the Jazz Band as well as the Baritone Saxophone and Bass Clarinet in small ensembles.

I have been selected multiple times for the District IV Honor Band as well as the Livingston Parish Honor Band. Recently this fall, I attended the McNeese State University Regional Honor Band and I was the 2025-2026 Drum Major for the Bands at Denham Springs. Through all this, I have held a job for almost a year in May.

Over the years, I have learned to be patient and independent. I live with 3 of my siblings and have learned to take care of them despite their incompetentness. In band, I have learned discipline and being prepared. In JROTC, I have learned how to teach, lead people, and complete tasks given by higher authority. My faith has taught me to keep going when everything is going wrong, to keep my faith in God because everything in this world will always come to an end. I am a product of everything and everyone who I have crossed paths with.
